About Me

I am an M.Sc. student at Sharif University of Technology, specializing in Digital VLSI System Design and Advanced Computer Architecture. My research interests include RTL design, hardware verification, custom FPGA architectures, and processor design. I am passionate about building efficient hardware systems from the ground up and rigorously verifying them against golden models.
